📐 Application maths (be honest with yourself)

25 quality applications/week → ~300 over 12 weeks. A realistic 8–12% interview rate for freshers → 25–35 interviews → 3–5 final rounds → 1–2 offers.

The people who "applied to 20 jobs and got nothing" usually applied to 20 jobs. Volume + tailoring + referrals is the formula.

🤝 Referral message that works

Hi [Name], I'm a frontend intern moving into full-stack (MERN + TypeScript). I built [project + live link], a job board with JWT auth and 40+ REST endpoints. I saw [Company] is hiring for [role]. Would you be open to referring me — or pointing me to the right person? Happy to send my resume. Thanks either way!

Resume upgrades (before you apply anywhere)

Lead with projects, not duties

"Built a MERN job board with JWT auth serving 40+ REST endpoints" beats "Worked on frontend tasks".

Put the stack in the top third

Recruiters and ATS scan for React, Node, MongoDB, TypeScript, Git. Make them impossible to miss.

Add numbers

Users, endpoints, screens, response time, test coverage, number of components.

One page, one column

No photos, no skill bars, no 'references available'. ATS-friendly PDF.

Link everything

Live demo + GitHub + LinkedIn in the header. If it isn't clickable, it doesn't exist.

Turn your internship into backend evidence

Ask for one API task or bug-fix sprint, then write the measurable result.
